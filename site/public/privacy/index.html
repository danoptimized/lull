Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Lull ("we", "us") is a productivity app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and — if you use Google sign-in — the basic profile Google returns (your Google account email). We store a user id.
  • Your content: the essentials you create, your focus sessions, and the points, streaks, and badges derived from them.
  • Device & settings: a per-install device id, platform, timezone, your notification preferences, and — only if you enable notifications — a push notification token.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and Lull contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, sync your essentials and sessions across your devices, compute your rewards, and (if enabled) send notifications.
  • To keep the rewards ledger honest and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes it

We use trusted providers to run the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and sync (stores your account and content).
  • Google — Google Sign-In (if you choose it) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (to deliver notifications, if you enable them).

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms; data is encrypted in transit.

Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Delete account). Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Children

Lull is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@lull.works.
