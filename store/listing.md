## Full description (Play ≤4000 / Apple description)
Lull is a quiet productivity app built around a simple daily rhythm.

Pick **three essentials** for the day — no endless lists, just the things that
matter. Give each one a number of focus sessions. Then work through them in
calm **25-minute focus sessions** with a gentle 5-minute break, on a timer
designed to be watched or ignored.

Showing up earns you something. Finished sessions, completed tasks, and full
days build an honest, tamper-proof rewards ledger — the start of rewards that
will one day reach beyond the app.

- **Three daily essentials** — a focused plan, not a backlog.
- **Calm 25/5 focus timer** — timestamp-anchored, so it stays accurate through
  backgrounding, sleep, and across devices.
- **Live across your devices** — start on one, watch it tick on another.
- **Gentle notifications** — opt-in, per category, never noisy.
- **A living, dark-first design** — a pre-dawn calm, soundscapes, and soft haptics.
- **Yours** — sign in with email or Google; delete your account and all data
  anytime, right from the app.

Free to use.
