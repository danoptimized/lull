## Common questions

**How does Lull work?**
Pick three essentials for the day, give each a number of focus sessions, then
work through them in calm 25-minute sessions. Finishing things builds your
rewards.

**Why didn't my focus session earn points?**
A session must be started while online and run without pausing to be creditable
— pausing keeps the timer running for you but doesn't mint a reward.

**How do I turn notifications on or off?**
Settings → Notifications. They're off until you enable a category, and you can
toggle each kind (timer, essentials, streak, rewards) per device.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your account and all data and
can't be undone.

**Which platforms?**
iPhone, Android, and the web today; macOS to follow.
